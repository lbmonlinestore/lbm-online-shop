CUSTOMER SUPPORT

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to common questions about shopping with LBM Online Shop.

How do I place an order?

Browse the products, open a product picture or name to view its gallery, and select the picture options you want. Add your selections to the cart, review the items and total, then choose Checkout via WhatsApp to send your order enquiry to our team.

Does adding an item to my cart mean I have ordered it?

No. Adding items to your cart does not complete an order or confirm a reservation of stock. Your order enquiry is sent when you proceed through WhatsApp, and our team will help confirm the order.

How do I choose a size, colour or picture?

Open the product gallery by tapping its picture or name. Select the picture or pictures you want. If size or colour choices are available, choose the correct option for each selected picture and enter the quantity before adding them to your cart.

Can I order more than one picture or option of the same product?

Yes. Where several product pictures are available, you can select more than one. Each selected picture can have its own size, colour and quantity. Your selections will appear separately in the cart for you to review.

What if a product is out of stock?

Products marked out of stock cannot be added to the cart. Available quantities are checked when you add selections and again during the order process. If you need help with availability, contact us on WhatsApp.

Which payment methods can I use?

LBM supports mobile money options including MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and Zamtel Money. Please contact our team through the official LBM WhatsApp number to confirm the payment instructions and recipient details for your order before sending money.

Do you deliver outside Lusaka?

LBM Online Shop serves customers across Zambia. Delivery availability, charges and estimated timing depend on the destination and order. Please confirm these details with our team before making payment.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Please read our Returns & Exchanges information for the applicable process and conditions. If you are unsure whether your order qualifies, contact us before sending the item back.

What is LBM Lay-Bay?

LBM Lay-Bay is a separate product-specific payment plan for eligible products. Its prices, deposits, deadlines and conditions differ from ordinary shop purchases. Visit the LBM Lay-Bay page and read the plan details before applying.

How can I contact LBM Online Shop?

Chat with us on WhatsApp at +260 969 034 955. You can also call 076 470 1367 or 097 500 4041. Our Technical Assistant can also help with common product and shopping questions.
