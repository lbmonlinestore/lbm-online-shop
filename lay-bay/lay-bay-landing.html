Important Information

  • Each Lay-Bay plan is linked to one specific product and its displayed Lay-Bay price.
  • The saving period is 30 calendar days from approval of the qualifying first deposit.
  • Customers may submit a cancellation request for administrator review.
  • No fixed or automatic percentage cancellation penalty is applied.
  • An administrator reviews each request individually and may determine a deduction only where there is a clear, disclosed and supportable basis under the programme terms and applicable law.
  • If a deduction is proposed, LBM will record its amount and specific reason. The remaining verified balance is the amount approved for refund.
  • Approved refunds are processed separately; the payment reference is recorded after the refund is actually sent.
  • Incomplete plans expire at the end of the 30-day saving period. Customers may then request a refund of the remaining verified balance for review.

Cancellation deductions are not automatic. Every decision must be individually reviewed, explained and recorded. LBM will not apply an undisclosed or arbitrary percentage penalty. These programme terms remain subject to applicable consumer-protection law.
