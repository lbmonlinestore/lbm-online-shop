Important Information

  • Each Lay-Bay plan is linked to one specific product and its displayed Lay-Bay price.
  • The saving period is 30 calendar days from approval of the qualifying first deposit.
  • Customers may submit a cancellation request for administrator review.
  • No fixed or automatic percentage cancellation penalty is applied.
  • An administrator reviews each request individually. No automatic percentage or flat cancellation penalty applies.
  • A deduction may be considered only for an actual, direct and non-recoverable cost incurred specifically for that customer's plan, where the cost category and calculation method were disclosed before the customer joined and the deduction is permitted by applicable law.
  • General business overhead, lost profit, arbitrary charges and unsupported estimates are not valid deduction reasons.
  • Any deduction must be itemised, supported by a record, and explained to the customer. The remaining verified balance is the amount approved for refund.
  • Approved refunds are processed separately; the payment reference is recorded after the refund is actually sent.
  • Incomplete plans expire at the end of the 30-day saving period. Customers may then request a refund of the remaining verified balance for review.

Cancellation deductions are not automatic. Every decision must be individually reviewed, explained and recorded. LBM will not apply an undisclosed or arbitrary percentage penalty. LBM will provide the customer with the deduction calculation and reason before processing the refund. These programme terms remain subject to applicable consumer-protection law.
