Important Information

  • Each savings plan is linked to one specific product.
  • The maximum saving period is 30 calendar days.
  • The first 14 days are the proposed penalty-free cancellation period.
  • From Day 15, a proposed 50% penalty applies only to qualifying later deposits, subject to the approved policy and applicable law.
  • Incomplete plans expire automatically on Day 30.
  • Expired plans require a customer refund request.
  • LBM aims to process approved refund requests within 5 working days.

These are proposed programme rules. Final terms, payment arrangements and cancellation deductions are subject to legal review and formal approval.
